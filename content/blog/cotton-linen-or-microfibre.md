## Thread count is mostly marketing

Above roughly 400, thread count is achieved by twisting thinner threads together and counting each one. A 300-count set of good long-staple cotton will usually feel better and last longer than an 800-count set of short fiber.

## Cotton: percale versus sateen

Percale is the crisp, matte, hotel-sheet feel and it breathes well. Sateen is smoother and slightly warmer with a light sheen. Neither is better; they are two different preferences, and it is worth knowing which one you already like before you buy.

## Linen: expensive, and worth it in summer

Linen is the coolest of the three and it softens for years instead of wearing out. It also arrives stiff, wrinkles permanently and costs the most, so it suits people who want a relaxed look and a cool bed rather than a smooth one.

## Microfibre: cheap, warm and it shows

Microfibre is the lowest price and the warmest, which is fine in a cold room and unpleasant in a warm one. It also pills over time. As a spare set or a guest bed it makes sense; as your everyday sheets it is a false economy.

## How to choose in thirty seconds

Sleep hot: percale cotton or linen. Sleep cold: sateen or microfibre. Want the least maintenance: percale cotton. Want it to look better in three years than it does new: linen.

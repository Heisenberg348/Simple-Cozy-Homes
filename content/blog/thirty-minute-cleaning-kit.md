## The six things in the basket

An all-purpose spray, a glass spray, a cream cleaner for sinks and tubs, a stack of microfibre cloths, a stiff detail brush, and a roll of bags. That covers every surface in a normal apartment.

## The thirty-minute route

Work top to bottom and in one direction: clear surfaces, spray the bathroom and let it sit, wipe the kitchen, come back to the bathroom, then vacuum last so everything you knocked down gets picked up.

## What the cleaning aisle wants you to sell you anyway

Room-specific sprays are mostly the same formula with different scents. Disposable wipes are convenient and expensive. Neither makes a home cleaner than a cloth and an all-purpose spray.

## Cloths do most of the work

Keep colors to jobs: one color for the bathroom, another for the kitchen, another for glass. Wash them together on a warm cycle without fabric softener, which clogs the fiber and ruins them.

## Restocking without overbuying

Buy refills rather than new trigger bottles, and only replace what actually ran out. The basket should stay the same size forever; that is the whole point of it.

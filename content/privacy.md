Simply Cozy Homes (“we”, “us”) operates this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have.

## Information we collect

- You give us: contact details, newsletter sign-up information, messages, and order information when you make a purchase.
- **Collected automatically:** IP address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, and time on site.
- **Cookies:** small files stored by your browser. We may use them to remember preferences, keep site features working and measure traffic.

## How we use it

- To operate and improve the site
- To send our newsletter, if you asked for it
- To answer your messages
- To understand which content is useful
- To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## Order And Payment Information

When you place an order, information such as your name, email, delivery address, order details and payment status may be processed to complete the purchase and provide support. Payment card data is handled by the payment provider used at checkout; do not send card details by email.

## Other services we use

- **Analytics** — to measure traffic
- **Email** — to store subscriber addresses and send the newsletter
- **Hosting** — Netlify, which logs standard request data for security and performance

## Your choices

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may stop working.
- Unsubscribe from any newsletter using the link at the bottom of the email.
- Ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to opt out of data sharing and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

## Data retention

We keep subscriber emails until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained per our analytics provider’s default period.

## Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

## Third-party links

Our pages may link to other websites or services. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date above.
